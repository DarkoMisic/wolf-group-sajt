## About

## Beskraj

Beskraj is a fruit brandy distillery in Batković and the company's principal
brand. The range consists of plum, quince, pear, apple and apricot brandies,
special releases and liqueurs. The entire process — fruit selection,
fermentation, distillation, ageing and bottling — takes place in-house.

Beskraj Signature is our programme of tasting and gastronomic events: wine,
brandy and food pairing.

A complex is under development in Batković, comprising the distillery, venues
for celebrations, tastings and business meetings, a terrace and accommodation.
