## 2. What personal data we process

When you contact us, we process the information you provide: your name, email address, telephone number if supplied, company name, message content and any attachments.

If your enquiry concerns our products, tastings, events or business cooperation, we also process the information needed to respond to that enquiry.

When you visit the website, its server and associated technical services may process your IP address, browser and device information, access times, pages visited and technical error information.

We obtain data directly from you or through the operation of the website. Please do not provide sensitive information or information about other people that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

## 3. Why we process data and our legal bases

We process personal data for the following purposes:

- Responding to enquiries and business communications: based on our legitimate interest in communicating with interested individuals and business contacts.
- Preparing an offer or arranging a service at your request: to take steps before entering into a contract, or to perform a contract where you are a party to it.
- Maintaining and protecting the website: based on our legitimate interest in keeping it operational, resolving errors and preventing misuse.
- Complying with legal obligations and requests from competent authorities: where we are legally required to do so.
- Protecting legal interests: where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
- Sending promotional communications, if you subscribe: based on your consent.

When relying on legitimate interests, we assess them against your rights and freedoms.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to promotional communications. Providing enquiry information is voluntary, but without the necessary contact details or other information we may be unable to respond or prepare the requested offer.

You can refuse or subsequently withdraw consent without losing access to the website’s basic content. Continuing to browse does not constitute consent.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions established by law, you may request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may object to direct marketing at any time, after which we will no longer use your data for that purpose.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

You also have rights concerning decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Send requests to [company@wolfdoo.com](mailto:company@wolfdoo.com). We respond without undue delay and no later than 30 days after receipt. Where the law permits an extension of a further 60 days, we will notify you within the initial period and explain the reasons. If we have reasonable doubts about the requester’s identity, we may request only the additional information needed to confirm it.
